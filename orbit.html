Mission log

Meet the spacecraft exploring our corner of the universe and the light arriving from beyond it.

Europa ClipperEn route · Jupiter system Launched in 2024

Evidence points to an ocean beneath Europa’s icy shell. Clipper will make close flybys to investigate whether this moon has the ingredients to support life.

NASA mission brief
James Webb Space TelescopeOperating · Sun–Earth L2 Launched in 2021

Webb gathers infrared light from a vantage point beyond the Moon. Its gold-coated mirrors bring distant galaxies, newborn stars, and the atmospheres of other worlds into view.

NASA mission brief
Mars Perseverance RoverOperating · Jezero Crater, Mars Launched in 2020

Rocks in an ancient Martian river delta preserve a record of flowing water. Perseverance examines them for traces of past microbial life and collects samples.

NASA mission brief
Parker Solar ProbeOperating · Solar corona Launched in 2018

Parker flies through the Sun’s outer atmosphere. Its instruments sample particles and magnetic fields to investigate how the corona heats up and the solar wind accelerates.
